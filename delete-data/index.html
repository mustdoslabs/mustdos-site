Data Deletion for MustDos

MustDos is provided by MustDos Labs LLC.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of data associated with MustDos, email us at support@mustdos.app with the subject line: Data Deletion Request.

Data stored on your device

MustDos stores your tasks locally on your device. You can delete your tasks in the app, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data from your device.

Crash and diagnostic data

MustDos uses Firebase Crashlytics to help identify and fix crashes. Crashlytics may collect crash logs, diagnostics, device/app information, and identifiers used for crash reporting.

Firebase Crashlytics retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting the removal process.

Data we may retain

We may retain limited information if required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate business records.
